# Training Exercises

1. List all datasets in the metadata catalogue and identify the steward for environmental samples.
2. Run the DQ validation endpoint for `health_observations` and note the overall score.
3. Query `vw_risk_analysis` for the latest period and list the top five communities by AP-EHRI.
4. In QGIS, style communities by risk band and overlay exposure sources.
5. Draft three policy actions based on high-risk districts with low lab access.
